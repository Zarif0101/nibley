## 1. What learners engage with
- Relatable study pain ("the never-ending cycle", rewatching a lecture 3 times) beats generic tips. [ANECDOTE: Virlo/Lemon8 pages, automated and unverified]
- ADHD study tips are a popular theme; top examples reach thousands of likes, most "study with me" clips get under 100. No reach data. [ANECDOTE]
- Tool reveals work when they show one concrete moment of "this solved my problem", not a feature list. [ANECDOTE]
- Saves/shares come from useful or recognisable things: a cheat sheet, a diagram, "this is me". Plain announcements get scrolled past. [ANECDOTE, consistent across sources]
- Study creators mostly break out on TikTok; Instagram study content is smaller. [ANECDOTE, unverified]

## 2. Reels and Instagram formats
- First seconds matter. Mosseri called them "super important" and said strong hooks use text and set expectations. [PLATFORM, 2024]. The "45% leave in 3 seconds" figure has no published source. [UNVERIFIED]
- Captions: Instagram's own Reels guide says add captions even if you use text overlays. [PLATFORM, via Social Media Today]. "Half watch with sound off" is quoted to Mosseri but the figure is unsourced. [UNVERIFIED]
- Length: no official ideal. Standard max is 90 s; 3 min only for some accounts. [PLATFORM, partly unverified] Your 20 s and 28 s are fine.
- Keep text away from the very top and bottom of the frame (UI covers it). Exact pixel numbers online are [UNVERIFIED].
- Reels win on reach; carousels win on engagement (saves, swipes). Buffer: Reels reach about 36% more than carousels and about 125% more than single images. [DATA, 2022-24]. Carousel engagement edge is small (1.38% vs 1.23% in one 10k-post study). [DATA]
- Single images are weakest and falling: Metricool says reach down about 22% and engagement rate down about 46% from early 2025 to early 2026. [DATA, vendor, 2026]
- Trial Reels show a Reel only to non-followers, so you can test hooks on strangers. [ANECDOTE/vendor]
- Cadence: no solid number found. Pick one you can keep (3 per week is realistic). [UNVERIFIED claims abound]
- Cover frame: make it readable as a tiny square: mascot + 3 words. (Design logic; no data found.)

## 3. X launch best practice
- Native video uploads get preferred over YouTube links. [ANECDOTE/vendor, consistent]
- Link penalty is disputed. Musk and X's product chief said links are not penalised any more (July 2026) [PLATFORM statement]; one 28k-post sample shows links at about 1.05x impressions [DATA, small accounts]. Safe move: demo video in the post, GitHub link in the same post or the first reply; either is fine.
- Thread shape that open-source guides repeat: problem, solution, demo, link. Demo under about 60 s with captions. [ANECDOTE, many sources are growth-service sellers]
- What makes people click GitHub: GIF/video at the top of the README, one-line install, honest requirements, screenshots. [ANECDOTE]
- Build in public works as small real updates ("fixed the voice bug, here is the before/after"). [ANECDOTE]
- Avoid: overclaiming, "revolutionary AI", stock gradients, emoji walls, fake numbers, asking for stars in every post. Stars are a side effect; real users who can install it are the goal. [ANECDOTE]
- Tue to Thu launch timing is repeated by guides. [ANECDOTE]

## 4. Mascots (Duolingo and one-person reality)
- Duolingo's social team is small (2 full-timers plus contractors reported, Mar 2025) and ships fast with a short approval chain. [ANECDOTE/press]
- Its formula: a clear personality (wacky on social, wholesome in-app), reacting to trends, fast replies to comments. [PLATFORM/press: Campaign Asia, Axios quotes]
- "Make a joke from the most relatable truth about your product" (Duolingo: guilt of skipping). [ANECDOTE]
- Realistic for you: one personality line ("shy but helpful"), 8 sounds you already have, 1 reaction clip per week, answer every comment in character. Skip big stunts and trend-chasing every day.
- Risk: Duolingo's tone is "unhinged"; Nibley is shy and cute. Do not copy chaos. Keep your own voice.

## 5. Recommendations

**Videos**
- Video 1 "Meet Nibley": KEEP. Change: make hook the ring being drawn at 0-2 s (the payoff), not "Hi I'm Nibley". Add "Windows + Claude" small tag on the end card. Reason: cute character plus payoff in the first seconds.
- Video 2 (hook-problem-solution): KEEP, but second priority. Change: cut "Searching kills your focus" lines to one beat; hannah/austin voices feel ad-like next to a cute mascot. Reason: "Rewatched 3 times?" is the relatable hook; the middle is generic. Make the requirement visible earlier.

**Posts**
1. Meet Nibley: REPLACE with Video 1's cover frame as a still. Reason: single images reach least; the video does this job.
2. How it works: MERGE into carousel (slide 2). Reason: carousels get swipes and saves.
3. Before/after: KEEP, as carousel hero slide. Reason: strongest visual proof; best save/share candidate.
4. Made for ADHD brains: CHANGE. See risks. Move to a personal founder post on X: "I have ADHD, I built this for how I study."
5. Moods grid: KEEP in carousel. Reason: shareable, "which one are you" invites comments.
6. Free and open source: CHANGE to a short X post plus README. Reason: dev-facing; will not interest Instagram learners.
7. It knows the basics (124 diagrams): CHANGE. Cut counts; show 3 real diagrams. Reason: "200+" claims invite scepticism and the count changes.
8. Save the good ones: KEEP as a short clip. Reason: concrete benefit.
9. Built in public: KEEP on X only. Reason: devs care about stack; learners do not.
10. Coming soon: DELAY by 2 to 4 weeks. Reason: announcing unbuilt features before the base works looks like vapourware.

**Could backfire**
- ADHD framing: "Made for ADHD brains" can read as a medical or cure claim, and some people dislike being used as a marketing hook. Say "made by a learner with ADHD" and describe design choices (short answers, one idea at a time). Do not say it "helps ADHD". Check how ADHD creators phrase it; no data found. [judgement]
- "Hold the hotkey": fine. Win + ` is two keys; say "Win + `" once clearly.
- "Needs Windows and a Claude subscription": big filter. Many students are on Mac or free tiers, so say it up front to avoid angry comments. It is also honest, which devs reward.
- "Reads your screen": expect privacy questions. Add one line: what is sent, and when. [judgement]
- "Explains simply / makes it make sense": do not promise better grades or accuracy. AI can be wrong; say "check important stuff".
- "Runs on your own subscription": confirm it fits Anthropic's terms for public distribution before posting. [needs checking]
- Videos showing a feature that is broken that day, or the voice bug.
- ElevenLabs licence for public use: still unchecked.
- Install guide and notes-folder setting: without them, stars arrive and installs fail.
- Spelling: "maths" vs "math". Pick by audience; you said general learners, so check where most followers are.
