# Newton's second law

## Simple idea
A bigger push makes something speed up faster, and a heavier thing needs a bigger push to speed up the same amount. Kick a football and it flies off; kick a parked car just as hard and it barely moves. The total (net) force decides how quickly the velocity changes.

## Key formulas
- F = m·a. F: net force (newtons, N), m: mass (kilograms, kg), a: acceleration (m/s²)
- a = F / m. The same law, solved for acceleration

## Picture it
Draw `block` on `ground`. Arrow from `block.right` pointing right, labelled "push F"; a shorter arrow from `block.left` pointing left, labelled "friction".
Label above `block.top`: "net force = push − friction".

## Common mistakes
- Using one force instead of the net force: add all forces, with their directions.
- Using grams: mass must be in kilograms before you use F = m·a.
- Thinking a moving object needs a force to keep moving: force changes motion, it does not keep it going.

## Check questions
1. A 2 kg trolley has a net force of 6 N on it. What is its acceleration?
   Answer: 3 m/s².
2. The same force pushes a 1 kg box and a 4 kg box. Which speeds up faster, and how many times faster?
   Answer: The 1 kg box, 4 times faster.
3. Why does a full shopping trolley need a harder push than an empty one to speed up the same?
   Answer: It has more mass, and a = F / m, so it needs more force for the same acceleration.

## Flashcards
Q: State Newton's second law in words.
A: Net force equals mass times acceleration.
Q: What is the unit of force?
A: The newton (N), equal to 1 kg·m/s².
Q: Net force doubles, mass stays the same. What happens to acceleration?
A: It doubles.
Q: What is the acceleration when all forces on an object balance?
A: Zero, so its velocity stays the same.
Q: Which way does acceleration point?
A: The same way as the net force.

## Root check
- free-body-diagrams: A book rests on a table. Name the two forces on it. Answer: Its weight down and the table's push (normal force) up.
- acceleration: A bike goes from 2 m/s to 8 m/s in 3 s. What is its acceleration? Answer: 2 m/s².
