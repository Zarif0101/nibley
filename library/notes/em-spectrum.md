# Electromagnetic waves

## Simple idea
Light is one member of a big family of waves. Radio, microwaves, visible light and X-rays are all the same kind of wave, made of changing electric and magnetic fields. They differ only in wavelength. All of them travel at the speed of light in empty space.

## Key formulas
- c = f·λ. c: speed of light (3.0 × 10⁸ m/s), f: frequency (hertz, Hz), λ: wavelength (metres, m)
- E = h·f. E: photon energy (joules, J), h: Planck's constant (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s), f: frequency (Hz)

## Common mistakes
- Thinking radio waves are slower than light: all EM waves travel at c in vacuum.
- Mixing up the order: from long to short wavelength is radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma.
- Thinking EM waves need air: they travel through empty space.

## Check questions
1. Name the EM waves in order of wavelength, longest first.
   Answer: Radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma.
2. A radio wave has frequency 1.0 × 10⁸ Hz. What is its wavelength?
   Answer: 3.0 m.
3. Why are gamma rays more dangerous than radio waves?
   Answer: Higher frequency means each photon carries much more energy.

## Flashcards
Q: What is an EM wave made of?
A: Oscillating electric and magnetic fields.
Q: What is the speed of EM waves in a vacuum?
A: 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s.
Q: Which has the longer wavelength, red or violet light?
A: Red.
Q: Are EM waves transverse or longitudinal?
A: Transverse.
Q: What is the relation between c, f and λ?
A: c = f·λ.
