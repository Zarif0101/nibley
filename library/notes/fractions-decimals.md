# Fractions and decimals

## Simple idea
Cut a pizza into 4 equal slices and eat 1: you ate 1/4. The bottom number says how many equal slices make the whole. The top number says how many you have. Divide top by bottom to get the decimal: 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25.

## Key formulas
- a/b: a is the parts you have, b is the equal parts in the whole (b is never 0)
- decimal = a ÷ b, so 3/4 = 0.75
- a/c + b/c = (a + b)/c. The bottoms must match first
- a/b × c/d = (a × c)/(b × d)

## Common mistakes
- Adding tops and bottoms: 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5. Make the bottoms equal first: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
- Thinking 0.3 is smaller than 0.25: write 0.30 and compare place by place.
- Thinking a bigger bottom means a bigger fraction: 1/8 is smaller than 1/4.

## Check questions
1. What does the bottom number of a fraction tell you?
   Answer: How many equal parts make the whole.
2. What is 1/2 + 1/4?
   Answer: 3/4.
3. Why is 1/8 smaller than 1/4?
   Answer: The whole is cut into more pieces, so each piece is smaller.

## Flashcards
Q: What is 1/2 as a decimal?
A: 0.5.
Q: What is 3/4 as a decimal?
A: 0.75.
Q: How do you turn a fraction into a decimal?
A: Divide the top number by the bottom number.
Q: How do you multiply two fractions?
A: Tops times tops, bottoms times bottoms.
Q: What is 1/5 as a decimal?
A: 0.2.
