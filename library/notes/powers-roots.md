# Powers and roots

## Simple idea
A square floor with sides of 3 tiles holds 3 × 3 = 9 tiles. We write that as 3², "3 squared". A square root goes the other way: √9 = 3, the side of a square with 9 tiles. The small raised number says how many times to multiply.

## Key formulas
- aⁿ = a × a × … (n copies). a: the base, n: the power
- a² is "squared", a³ is "cubed"
- √x: the number that times itself gives x, so √16 = 4
- a⁰ = 1 (for a not 0), and 10³ = 1000

## Picture it
Draw `cuboid`. Label `cuboid.length-mid` "3", `cuboid.depth-mid` "3" and `cuboid.height-mid` "3".
Label `cuboid.top-center` "3³ = 27 cubes".

## Common mistakes
- Writing 3² = 6: it means 3 × 3 = 9, not 3 × 2.
- Writing (−3)² = −9: negative times negative is positive, so it is 9.
- Writing √(9 + 16) = 3 + 4: add first, √25 = 5.

## Check questions
1. What is √49?
   Answer: 7.
2. What is 2³?
   Answer: 8.
3. Why is (−4)² equal to 16 and not −16?
   Answer: A negative times a negative is positive.
