# Organic chemistry basics

## Simple idea
Carbon is like a building block with four hooks. It joins to itself and to other atoms, making chains, like Lego. Organic compounds are named by how long the chain is and by the functional group, the small part that decides how it reacts.

## Key formulas
- Chain names: 1 meth, 2 eth, 3 prop, 4 but, 5 pent, 6 hex
- Carbon forms 4 bonds
- Alkane ending -ane: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂. n: number of carbons
- Functional groups: C=C alkene, –OH alcohol, –COOH carboxylic acid

## Picture it
Draw `carbon-chain`. Label `carbon-chain.chain-start` "C1" and `carbon-chain.chain-end` "C5, five-carbon chain". Circle `carbon-chain.group` and label it "functional group: reacts here".

## Common mistakes
- Counting the wrong number of carbons: count the longest unbroken chain.
- Giving carbon more or fewer than 4 bonds: always 4 in total.
- Mixing up the stem and the ending: stem gives length, ending gives family.

## Check questions
1. Name the chain with 3 carbons and only single bonds.
   Answer: Propane.
2. How many bonds does each carbon atom make?
   Answer: Four.
3. Why do compounds with the same chain but a different functional group react differently?
   Answer: The functional group is the part that takes part in the reaction.

## Flashcards
Q: What is a functional group?
A: The part of a molecule that decides how it reacts.
Q: What prefix means 4 carbons?
A: But-.
Q: What prefix means 2 carbons?
A: Eth-.
Q: What is the functional group of an alcohol?
A: –OH.
Q: How many bonds does carbon form?
A: Four.
