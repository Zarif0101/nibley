# Refraction

## Simple idea
Push a shopping trolley from smooth floor onto grass at a slant: the wheel that hits the grass slows first, so the trolley turns. Light does this going from air into glass or water. It slows down and bends. Slower material means bending toward the normal.

## Key formulas
- n = c / v. n: refractive index (no unit), c: speed of light in vacuum (3.0 × 10⁸ m/s), v: speed in the material (m/s)
- n₁·sinθ₁ = n₂·sinθ₂. Snell's law. θ₁, θ₂: angles from the normal (degrees), n₁, n₂: refractive indices

## Picture it
Draw `refraction`. Label `refraction.angle-in` "bigger angle in air" and `refraction.angle-out` "smaller angle in glass". Dashed line from `refraction.normal-top` to `refraction.normal-bottom` labelled "normal", with `refraction.hit-point` marked "light slows here".

## Common mistakes
- Measuring angles from the surface: measure from the normal.
- Thinking light bends away going into glass: into a slower material it bends toward the normal.
- Believing n can be below 1: n = c / v, and light is never faster than c.

## Check questions
1. Glass has n = 1.5. How fast does light travel in it?
   Answer: 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s.
2. Light goes from air into water. Does it bend toward or away from the normal?
   Answer: Toward the normal.
3. Why does a straw in a glass of water look bent?
   Answer: Light from the straw bends as it leaves the water, so your eye places it elsewhere.

## Flashcards
Q: What is refraction?
A: Light bending as it changes speed between materials.
Q: What is the refractive index of a vacuum?
A: Exactly 1.
Q: State Snell's law.
A: n₁·sinθ₁ = n₂·sinθ₂.
Q: What happens to light's speed going from air to glass?
A: It slows down.
Q: Does frequency change when light enters glass?
A: No. Speed and wavelength change, frequency stays the same.

## Root check
- reflection-mirrors: A ray hits a mirror at 30° to the normal. At what angle does it leave? Answer: 30°.
- trig-ratios: What is sin 30°? Answer: 0.5.
