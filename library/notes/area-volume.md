# Area, circles and volume

## Simple idea
Area counts how many 1 by 1 squares cover a flat shape, like floor tiles. Volume counts how many 1 by 1 by 1 cubes fill a solid, like sugar cubes in a box. A rectangle 3 by 2 holds 6 squares. A box 2 by 3 by 4 holds 24 cubes.

## Key formulas
- Rectangle: A = l·w. l: length (m), w: width (m), A: area (m²)
- Triangle: A = ½·b·h. b: base (m), h: height straight up from the base (m)
- Circle: A = π·r², C = 2·π·r. r: radius (m), π ≈ 3.14, C: edge length (m)
- Box: V = l·w·h. h: height (m), V: volume (m³)

## Picture it
Draw `cuboid`. Label `cuboid.length-mid` "l", `cuboid.depth-mid` "w", `cuboid.height-mid` "h" and `cuboid.top-center` "V = l × w × h".
Draw `circle`. Label `circle.radius-mid` "r" and `circle.center` "A = π r²".

## Common mistakes
- Putting the diameter into π·r²: the radius is half the diameter.
- Giving volume in m²: volume units are cubed, m³.
- Forgetting the ½ for a triangle, or using the slanted side as the height.

## Check questions
1. What is the area of a rectangle?
   Answer: Length times width.
2. A box is 2 m by 3 m by 4 m. What is its volume?
   Answer: 24 m³.
3. Why is area measured in squared units?
   Answer: You are counting squares, each 1 unit by 1 unit.

## Flashcards
Q: What is the area of a circle?
A: π·r², with r the radius.
Q: What is the circumference of a circle?
A: 2·π·r.
Q: What is the area of a triangle?
A: Half times base times height.
Q: How is diameter linked to radius?
A: Diameter = 2 × radius.
Q: What is the circle area for r = 2 m, using π ≈ 3.14?
A: About 12.6 m².
