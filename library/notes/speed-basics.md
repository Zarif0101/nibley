# Speed, distance and time

## Simple idea
Speed tells you how far you travel in each second. If you walk 2 metres every second, your speed is 2 m/s. Go faster and you cover more distance in the same time. Know any two of speed, distance and time and you can find the third.

## Key formulas
- v = d / t. v: speed (m/s), d: distance (m), t: time (s)
- d = v·t. Distance from speed and time
- t = d / v. Time from distance and speed
- km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s. Use this to change units

## Common mistakes
- Dividing the wrong way round: speed is distance divided by time, not time divided by distance.
- Mixing km with seconds: change to metres and seconds, or km and hours.
- Averaging two speeds for a whole trip: use total distance divided by total time.

## Check questions
1. A runner covers 150 m in 30 s. What is the speed?
   Answer: 5 m/s.
2. A cyclist rides at 3 m/s for 20 s. How far is that?
   Answer: 60 m.
3. Why is speed in m/s and not just m?
   Answer: Speed is distance per second, so it needs distance over time.

## Flashcards
Q: What is the formula for speed?
A: Speed = distance ÷ time.
Q: What is the SI unit of speed?
A: Metres per second (m/s).
Q: How do you find distance?
A: Distance = speed × time.
Q: Change 72 km/h into m/s.
A: 20 m/s.
Q: A car goes 100 m in 10 s. What is its speed?
A: 10 m/s.
