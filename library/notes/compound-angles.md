# Compound and double angles

## Simple idea
Adding angles does not mean adding their sines: sin(A + B) is not sin A + sin B. Test it with A = B = 90°. The sine of 180° is 0, but 1 + 1 = 2. Instead there are set formulas that mix sines and cosines. Setting B = A gives the double angle formulas.

## Key formulas
- sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B. A, B: angles (degrees or radians)
- cos(A + B) = cos A cos B − sin A sin B
- sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A
- cos 2A = cos²A − sin²A = 1 − 2sin²A = 2cos²A − 1

## Picture it
Draw `unit-circle`. Label the angle at `unit-circle.angle` "A". Put a dot at `unit-circle.point` labelled "(cos A, sin A)".
Add a note beside `unit-circle.right`: "turn A again to get 2A".

## Common mistakes
- Splitting sin(A + B) into sin A + sin B: use the full formula.
- Mixing the sign in cos(A + B): cosine formula has a minus, sine formula has a plus.
- Writing sin 2A = 2 sin A: it is 2 sin A cos A.

## Check questions
1. If sin A = 3/5 and cos A = 4/5, what is sin 2A?
   Answer: 2 × 3/5 × 4/5 = 24/25.
2. Use cos 2A = 1 − 2sin²A with A = 30° to find cos 60°.
   Answer: 1 − 2 × (1/2)² = 1/2.
3. Why is sin(A + B) not equal to sin A + sin B?
   Answer: With A = B = 90°, sin 180° = 0 but the sum is 2.

## Flashcards
Q: Write sin(A + B).
A: sin A cos B + cos A sin B.
Q: Write cos(A + B).
A: cos A cos B − sin A sin B.
Q: Write sin 2A.
A: 2 sin A cos A.
Q: Write cos 2A using only cosine.
A: 2cos²A − 1.
Q: Write cos 2A using only sine.
A: 1 − 2sin²A.

## Root check
- trig-identities: If cos θ = 0.6 and θ is acute, what is sin θ? Answer: 0.8, because sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
