# Tissues, organs and organ systems

## Simple idea
Cells of one kind team up to make a tissue, like muscle. Different tissues team up to make an organ, like the heart. Organs that work on one big job make an organ system, like the circulatory system. It is like players, a team, then a whole league.

## Key formulas
- cells → tissues → organs → organ systems → organism. The order from smallest to biggest

## Picture it

## Common mistakes
- Mixing up the order: always go cell, tissue, organ, organ system.
- Thinking an organ is one tissue: an organ has several tissues, like muscle and blood.
- Thinking the heart is a whole system: it is one organ in the circulatory system.

## Check questions
1. Put in order, smallest first: organ, cell, tissue.
   Answer: Cell, tissue, organ.
2. Name one organ in the digestive system.
   Answer: Stomach, or small intestine, or liver.
3. Why is the heart an organ and not just a tissue?
   Answer: It is made of several tissues, such as muscle and blood vessels, working together.

## Flashcards
Q: What is a tissue?
A: A group of similar cells doing the same job.
Q: What is an organ?
A: A group of different tissues working together on one job.
Q: What is an organ system?
A: A group of organs working together on a big job.
Q: Which organ system carries blood around the body?
A: The circulatory system.
Q: What does the heart do?
A: It pumps blood around the body.
