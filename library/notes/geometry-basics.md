# Angles and shapes

## Simple idea
An angle measures turning. Spin all the way round and you turn 360°. Half a spin is 180°, a straight line. A quarter spin is 90°, like the corner of a sheet of paper. The three angles inside any triangle always add up to 180°.

## Key formulas
- Triangle: a + b + c = 180°. a, b, c: the three angles (degrees, °)
- Angles on a straight line add to 180°
- Angles around a point add to 360°

## Picture it

Under it write "50 + 60 + ? = 180". On `angle`, label `angle.arc-mid` "angle".

## Common mistakes
- Thinking longer sides mean a bigger angle: an angle measures turning, not length.
- Using 360° for a triangle: it is 180°.
- Misreading a protractor: check if the angle is under or over 90° first.

## Check questions
1. What do the angles in a triangle add up to?
   Answer: 180°.
2. A triangle has angles 50° and 60°. What is the third?
   Answer: 70°.
3. Why is a right angle 90°?
   Answer: It is a quarter of a full turn, and 360° ÷ 4 = 90°.

## Flashcards
Q: How many degrees in a right angle?
A: 90°.
Q: How many degrees on a straight line?
A: 180°.
Q: How many degrees in a full turn?
A: 360°.
Q: What is an acute angle?
A: An angle smaller than 90°.
Q: What is an obtuse angle?
A: An angle between 90° and 180°.
