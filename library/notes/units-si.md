# SI units

## Simple idea
A measurement is a number plus a unit. "5" means nothing, but "5 metres" does. Scientists share one set of units, called SI, so everyone means the same thing. The three you use most: metre for length, kilogram for mass, second for time.

## Key formulas
- 1 km = 1000 m. km: kilometres, m: metres
- 1 kg = 1000 g. kg: kilograms, g: grams
- 1 min = 60 s and 1 h = 3600 s. min: minutes, h: hours, s: seconds

## Common mistakes
- Writing a number with no unit: always say what was measured (5 m, 5 s).
- Mixing units in one sum, like grams and kilograms: convert to one unit first.
- Converting the wrong way: going from big units to small ones makes the number bigger.

## Check questions
1. What is the SI unit of mass?
   Answer: The kilogram (kg).
2. Change 2.5 km into metres.
   Answer: 2500 m.
3. Why do we write a unit after a number?
   Answer: The number alone does not say what was measured, like 5 metres or 5 seconds.
