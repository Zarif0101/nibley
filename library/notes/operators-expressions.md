# Operators and expressions

## Simple idea
Operators are the action symbols: + adds, - subtracts, * multiplies, / divides. An expression like price * 2 + 1 is a small calculation the computer works out into one value, like a till adding up your shopping. The same order rules as maths apply: times and divide before plus and minus.

## Key formulas
- + add, - subtract, * multiply, / divide, % remainder, ** power (in Python).
- Order: brackets, then ** , then * / %, then + -.

## Picture it
Draw `function-box`. Label `function-box.input`: "3 + 4 * 2", `function-box.output`: "11".

## Common mistakes
- Working left to right only: 3 + 4 * 2 is 11, not 14.
- Using x for multiply: code uses *.
- Forgetting % gives the remainder, not a percentage.

## Check questions
1. What is 3 + 4 * 2 in code?
   Answer: 11.
2. What is 7 % 3?
   Answer: 1, the remainder.
3. What is (3 + 4) * 2?
   Answer: 14.

## Flashcards
Q: What symbol multiplies in code?
A: *
Q: What does % do?
A: Gives the remainder after dividing.
Q: What is an expression?
A: A combination of values and operators that works out to one value.
Q: What is 2 ** 3 in Python?
A: 8.
Q: Which is done first, + or *?
A: *, unless brackets say otherwise.

## Root check
- variables-values: What does score = 10 do? Answer: Gives the name score to the value 10.
- arithmetic-basics: What is 2 + 3 × 4? Answer: 14.
