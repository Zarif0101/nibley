# Reading graphs and slope

## Simple idea
A graph shows how one thing changes as another changes, like how far you have walked as time passes. The slope tells how steep the line is. Steeper means the thing changes faster. Slope is how far the line goes up for each step across.

## Key formulas
- slope m = rise ÷ run = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Rise: change up, run: change across
- y = mx + c. m: slope, c: where the line crosses the y-axis

## Picture it
Draw `graph-line`. Label `graph-line.rise-mid` "rise" and `graph-line.run-mid` "run".
Label `graph-line.y-intercept` "c" and `graph-line.line-mid` "slope = rise ÷ run".

## Common mistakes
- Flipping it to run ÷ rise: slope is rise over run.
- Mixing the order of points: subtract in the same order on top and bottom.
- Judging slope by looks: use the numbers on the axes.

## Check questions
1. What is the slope formula in words?
   Answer: Rise divided by run.
2. A line goes through (1, 2) and (3, 8). What is its slope?
   Answer: 3.
3. Why does a steeper line have a bigger slope?
   Answer: It rises more for the same step across.

## Flashcards
Q: What is the slope of a flat line?
A: 0.
Q: What does a line going downhill have?
A: A negative slope.
Q: What is the slope of y = 2x + 1?
A: 2.
Q: Where does y = 2x + 1 cross the y-axis?
A: At y = 1.
Q: What does c mean in y = mx + c?
A: The y-intercept, where the line crosses the y-axis.

## Root check
- coordinates: Which direction do you go first to plot (2, 5)? Answer: 2 across, then 5 up.
- ratios-percentages: Write the ratio 6:3 in simplest form. Answer: 2:1.
