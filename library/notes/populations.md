# Population growth

## Simple idea
Start with a few rabbits in a big field. With plenty of food they breed fast, and the population grows faster and faster. But food, space and water run out. Growth slows and then levels off. The most the habitat can support for a long time is the carrying capacity.

## Key formulas
- Population change = births + immigration − deaths − emigration
- Early growth is exponential: the population multiplies by the same factor each time period
- S-shaped (logistic) curve levels off at the carrying capacity, K

## Common mistakes
- Thinking growth never stops: limits like food and space cap it.
- Forgetting migration: immigration adds and emigration removes individuals.
- Thinking the population stays exactly at K: it often wobbles around it.

## Check questions
1. What is carrying capacity?
   Answer: The largest population a habitat can support over time.
2. 50 mice double every month. How many after 3 months?
   Answer: 400.
3. Why does growth slow down near the carrying capacity?
   Answer: Food and space run short, so deaths rise and births fall.

## Flashcards
Q: What is a population?
A: A group of one species living in one place.
Q: What does immigration mean?
A: Individuals moving into an area.
Q: Name one limiting factor.
A: Food, water, space, predators or disease.
Q: What shape is a logistic growth curve?
A: An S shape.
Q: What is exponential growth?
A: Growth that multiplies by the same factor each period.
