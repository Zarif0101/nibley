# Ratio, proportion and percentages

## Simple idea
A recipe uses 2 cups of flour for every 1 cup of sugar. That is a ratio of 2:1. For 4 cups of flour you need 2 cups of sugar. "Per cent" means out of 100, so 50% is 50 out of 100, which is half.

## Key formulas
- Ratio a:b. a and b are amounts of the same kind, in the same unit
- Share by ratio: one part = total ÷ (a + b)
- Percent of an amount = (p ÷ 100) × amount. p: the percent
- Percentage = part ÷ whole × 100

## Common mistakes
- Mixing units: 1 m : 50 cm. Change to 100 cm : 50 cm = 2:1 first.
- Reading 1:3 as one third: it is 1 part out of 4, so one quarter.
- Forgetting the ÷ 100: 20% of 50 is 0.2 × 50 = 10.

## Check questions
1. What does "per cent" mean?
   Answer: Out of 100.
2. Share 20 sweets in the ratio 2:3. How many does each get?
   Answer: 8 and 12.
3. Why is 25% the same as 1/4?
   Answer: 25 out of 100 simplifies to 1 out of 4.

## Flashcards
Q: What is 50% as a fraction?
A: 1/2.
Q: How do you find 10% of an amount?
A: Divide it by 10.
Q: What is 25% as a decimal?
A: 0.25.
Q: In the ratio 3:1, how many equal parts are there in total?
A: 4.
Q: How do you turn part and whole into a percentage?
A: Part ÷ whole × 100.
