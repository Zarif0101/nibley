# Arithmetic basics

## Simple idea
You have £5 and spend £8, so you owe £3. That is a negative number. When a sum has several steps, do brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −, working left to right. So 2 + 3 × 4 is 2 + 12 = 14.

## Key formulas
- Order: brackets, then × and ÷, then + and −, left to right
- (−) × (−) = (+), and (−) × (+) = (−). The same signs give plus, different signs give minus
- Subtracting a negative adds: 5 − (−2) = 7

## Common mistakes
- Going strictly left to right: 2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20. Multiply first.
- Thinking minus times minus is minus: −2 × −3 = 6, a plus.
- Dropping the sign: 3 − 7 = −4, not 4. Count past zero.

## Check questions
1. What is −2 × −3?
   Answer: 6.
2. What is 7 − 10?
   Answer: −3.
3. Why is 2 + 3 × 4 equal to 14 and not 20?
   Answer: Multiplying comes first: 3 × 4 = 12, then add 2.

## Flashcards
Q: Which comes first, × or +?
A: Multiplication (and division) comes before addition (and subtraction).
Q: What is a negative times a negative?
A: A positive.
Q: What is 5 − 8?
A: −3.
Q: What do you do first in a sum with brackets?
A: Work out the brackets.
Q: What is −4 + 9?
A: 5.
