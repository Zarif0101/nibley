# Energy basics

## Simple idea
Energy is what lets things happen: moving, heating, lighting up. It never disappears; it only changes form. Hold a ball high and it stores energy. Drop it and that stored energy turns into movement energy. A faster or heavier thing has more movement energy.

## Key formulas
- KE = ½·m·v². KE: movement energy (joules, J), m: mass (kg), v: speed (m/s)
- GPE = m·g·h. GPE: height energy (J), m: mass (kg), g: 9.8 N/kg, h: height (m)

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting to square the speed: v² means v × v, not v × 2.
- Forgetting the ½ in KE = ½·m·v².
- Saying energy gets "used up": it changes form, the total stays the same.

## Check questions
1. A 2 kg ball moves at 3 m/s. What is its movement energy?
   Answer: 9 J (½ × 2 × 3²).
2. A 1 kg book is lifted 2 m. What height energy does it gain?
   Answer: 19.6 J (1 × 9.8 × 2).
3. Why does a ball speed up as it rolls down a ramp?
   Answer: Its height energy changes into movement energy.

## Flashcards
Q: What is the SI unit of energy?
A: The joule (J).
Q: State the idea of energy conservation.
A: Energy is never made or destroyed, only changed from one form to another.
Q: What is the formula for movement energy?
A: KE = ½·m·v².
Q: What is the formula for height energy?
A: GPE = m·g·h.
Q: Speed doubles. What happens to movement energy?
A: It becomes 4 times bigger.
