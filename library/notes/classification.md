# Classification of living things

## Simple idea
There are millions of kinds of living things, so scientists sort them into groups by shared features, like sorting a music app into genres and then artists. The biggest group is the kingdom and the smallest is the species. Each species gets a two-part Latin name.

## Key formulas
- levels, biggest to smallest: kingdom → phylum → class → order → family → genus → species
- two-part name: genus + species, like Homo sapiens

## Common mistakes
- Mixing up the order of levels: kingdom is biggest, species is smallest.
- Thinking looks alone decide groups: shared features and DNA both count.
- Writing the name wrongly: genus gets a capital, species is lower case.

## Check questions
1. What is the largest group in classification?
   Answer: The kingdom.
2. In Homo sapiens, which word is the genus?
   Answer: Homo.
3. Why do two species in the same genus count as close relatives?
   Answer: They share many features and a recent common ancestor.

## Flashcards
Q: What is a species?
A: A group that can breed and have fertile young.
Q: Name three kingdoms.
A: Animals, plants and fungi.
Q: What does a binomial name have?
A: Two parts: genus, then species.
Q: What does a branch point on a tree show?
A: Where two groups split from a common ancestor.
Q: Who made the two-part naming system popular?
A: Carl Linnaeus.
