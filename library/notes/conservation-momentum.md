# Conservation of momentum

## Simple idea
When two things bump and nothing outside pushes them, the total "oomph" of motion stays the same. A skater pushes a friend on ice: the friend glides one way, the skater slides back the other way. Momentum is mass times velocity, and one object's gain is the other's loss.

## Key formulas
- p = m·v. p: momentum (kg·m/s), m: mass (kg), v: velocity (m/s)
- m₁·u₁ + m₂·u₂ = m₁·v₁ + m₂·v₂. m: masses (kg), u: velocities before (m/s), v: velocities after (m/s)

## Picture it
Draw two `ball` shapes side by side. Arrow from the left ball's `ball.right` pointing right, labelled "before: moving". Arrow from the right ball's `ball.left` pointing right, labelled "after: both moving".
Write above: "total momentum before = after".

## Common mistakes
- Ignoring direction: pick one way as positive, and give the other way a minus sign.
- Using it when an outside force acts (like friction): it only works for a closed system.
- Mixing grams and kilograms: change mass to kg first.

## Check questions
1. What must be true about outside forces for momentum to be conserved?
   Answer: There must be no net outside force.
2. A 2 kg trolley at 3 m/s hits a 1 kg trolley at rest and they stick. How fast do they move together?
   Answer: Momentum 6 kg·m/s over 3 kg gives 2 m/s.
3. Why does a gun recoil backwards when it fires a bullet forwards?
   Answer: Total momentum was zero, so the gun gets equal momentum the opposite way.

## Flashcards
Q: What is the formula for momentum?
A: p = m·v, mass times velocity.
Q: What is the unit of momentum?
A: kg·m/s.
Q: When is total momentum conserved?
A: When no net outside force acts on the system.
Q: Is momentum a vector or a scalar?
A: A vector. It has a direction.
Q: In a collision, what happens to the total momentum?
A: It is the same before and after.

## Root check
- momentum-impulse: What is the momentum of a 5 kg cart moving at 4 m/s? Answer: 20 kg·m/s.
- newtons-third-law: You push a wall with 50 N. What force does the wall put on you? Answer: 50 N, back at you.
