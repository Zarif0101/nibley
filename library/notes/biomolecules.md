# Biological molecules

## Simple idea
Living things are built from four big molecule types. Carbohydrates give quick energy, like bread. Lipids (fats) store energy. Proteins build and repair, like muscle. Nucleic acids, DNA and RNA, hold the instructions. Big molecules are made of many small ones joined in chains.

## Key formulas
- carbohydrates: many sugars (like glucose) joined together
- proteins: many amino acids joined together
- lipids: 1 glycerol + 3 fatty acids
- DNA base pairs: A with T, C with G

## Picture it
Draw `dna-helix`. Label `dna-helix.strand-1` and `dna-helix.strand-2`: "two strands". Label `dna-helix.base-pair`: "A-T or C-G".

## Common mistakes
- Mixing up the building blocks: proteins are amino acids, carbohydrates are sugars.
- Pairing A with C: A pairs with T, and C pairs with G.
- Thinking all fats are bad: lipids store energy and make cell membranes.

## Check questions
1. What small units make up a protein?
   Answer: Amino acids.
2. Which base pairs with G in DNA?
   Answer: C.
3. Why is glucose a good fuel for cells?
   Answer: Cells can break it down quickly to release energy.

## Flashcards
Q: Which molecule type gives quick energy?
A: Carbohydrates.
Q: What is DNA's shape?
A: A double helix, two twisted strands.
Q: What do lipids do?
A: Store energy, insulate, and form cell membranes.
Q: Name the building block of nucleic acids.
A: Nucleotides.
Q: Name a job of proteins.
A: Growth and repair, hormones, or enzymes.
