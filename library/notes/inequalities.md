# Inequalities

## Simple idea
An inequality says one side is bigger or smaller, like "you must be at least 16 to ride". The answer is a whole range of numbers, not just one. You solve it like an equation, with one big catch: if you multiply or divide by a negative number, the sign flips.

## Key formulas
- <: less than. >: greater than. ≤: less than or equal to. ≥: greater than or equal to
- Multiply or divide by a negative: flip the sign (−2x > 6 becomes x < −3)
- x² < k (k > 0): −√k < x < √k. x² > k: x < −√k or x > √k

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting to flip the sign when dividing by a negative: flip it.
- Mixing open and closed circles: < and > are open, ≤ and ≥ are filled.
- Giving one range for x² < 9: it needs both ends, −3 < x < 3.

## Check questions
1. Solve 2x + 1 < 7.
   Answer: x < 3.
2. Solve −2x > 6.
   Answer: x < −3.
3. Why is the circle open for x < 3?
   Answer: 3 itself is not included in the answer.

## Flashcards
Q: What does ≤ mean?
A: Less than or equal to.
Q: When do you flip the inequality sign?
A: When multiplying or dividing by a negative number.
Q: Open circle on a number line means what?
A: The end number is not included.
Q: Solve x² < 9.
A: −3 < x < 3.
Q: What does the answer to an inequality look like?
A: A range of numbers, not a single value.

## Root check
- linear-equations: Solve 3x − 4 = 11. Answer: x = 5.
- quadratics: Solve x² − 4 = 0. Answer: x = 2 or x = −2.
