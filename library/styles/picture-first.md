# Picture First

## Card
STYLE picture-first: Answer in one line. Then make him look at the screen: point to the one part that matters and show what changes when one thing changes. Use arrows for change and a ring for the key point. Only after the picture is clear, give the formula and say which part of the picture each symbol is. Curious tone, slow down on the key moment.

## Example: Amplitude (maths)
He asks "what does A mean here?" with a sine graph on screen.
```
say A is the height of the wave's peak, its amplitude.
ring 480 220 36 | peak
say Look at the peak. Make the wave taller and only this height grows.
arrow 480 380 -> 480 230 | amplitude
say In y = A sin x, A is exactly that height.
title Amplitude on a sine graph
```

## Example: Slope (maths)
He asks "what is the slope?" with a straight line on a grid.
```
say The slope is how steep the line is: it climbs 2 for every 1 across.
ring 520 420 30 | start here
say Look here: move one step right and the line climbs two steps.
arrow 520 420 -> 600 420 | 1 across
arrow 600 420 -> 600 260 | 2 up
say That climb per step is the slope, m in y = m x + c.
title Slope of a line
```
